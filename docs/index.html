FAQ

Common questions

What is Tender Reach?

Tender Reach is a free WhatsApp-based assistant and website that helps Zimbabwean businesses discover, understand, and prepare for public procurement (tender) opportunities. We turn dense tender documents into plain-language summaries and checklists.

How do I find tenders in Zimbabwe?

Public tenders in Zimbabwe are published on PRAZ's electronic Government Procurement (eGP) bulletin board. Tender Reach maintains a continuously refreshed, searchable index of that same public data on our Knowledge Centre page, so you can search and filter without navigating the original portal directly.

What is PRAZ?

PRAZ is the Procurement Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe — the government body that regulates and oversees public procurement under the Public Procurement and Disposal of Public Assets Act. Tender Reach is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by PRAZ.

Is Tender Reach free to use?

Yes. Tender discovery, plain-language summaries, deadline guidance, and our WhatsApp assistant are free, with no paywall on the core supplier support. Read more in our About page.

How do I register as a government supplier in Zimbabwe?

Supplier registration happens directly through PRAZ's eGP system. Tender Reach can walk you through what's typically required in plain language via WhatsApp — message us to get started.

Does Tender Reach replace PRAZ's eGP system?

No. Tender Reach is a companion layer built on top of PRAZ's already-public tender data, aimed at making it easier to search and understand. Bid submission and official supplier registration still happen through PRAZ's own eGP system.
